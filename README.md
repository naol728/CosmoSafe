## 🌍 About

**CosmoSafe** is a next-generation **climate and safety intelligence platform** that helps users monitor environmental conditions, predict natural disasters, and analyze carbon emissions in real-time.  
It brings together **data visualization, AI-driven insights, and environmental awareness** — all wrapped in a modern, elegant interface.

> _Our goal: Empower people and policymakers to make smarter, safer, and more sustainable decisions._

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🌦️ **Weather Intelligence** — Real-time 7-day forecast with visual charts  
- 🌊 **Flood Risk Monitoring** — River discharge and flood probability tracking  
- 🌋 **Disaster Tracking** — Live global natural disaster alerts and insights  
- 🌱 **Carbon Emission Dashboard** — Global CO₂ levels and environmental trends  
- 📍 **Geo-Aware Insights** — Personalized data based on your location  
- 💡 **Modern UI/UX** — Built with **React**, **Shadcn UI**, and **Tailwind CSS**
